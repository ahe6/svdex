# Privacy Policy

Effective {{effective_date}}

{{operator_intro}} This policy explains how we handle information when you use [svdex.com](https://svdex.com), its community features and our private-access application. Contact [austin@svdex.com](mailto:austin@svdex.com) with privacy questions or requests.

## Information we collect

**Accounts and profiles.** We collect your email address, username, authentication information and any name, photo, biography, website or other profile information you provide. If you sign in with Google, we receive basic identity information such as your Google account identifier, name, email and profile picture. Google sign-in does not give SVDEX access to your Gmail, Drive or Calendar.

**Community activity.** We store topics, replies, uploads, reactions, bookmarks, event participation, chat messages, direct messages, reports and account preferences. We also keep records needed for moderation, access decisions and account security.

**Private-access applications.** Our application forms ask for your name, SVDEX account email, country, whether you apply individually or for an entity, your stated accreditation basis, investment experience, broad interests, relationship context and confirmations. Older Google Forms applications may also contain financial ranges and intended investment amounts. Our Tally questionnaire does not request exact balances or verification documents. We receive submitted answers and follow-up correspondence for staff review. Do not provide passwords, account numbers, identity documents, tax returns or financial statements through these forms.

**Company applications and nonbinding interest.** Company/fund applications are private Discourse membership requests containing the organization name, representative, description, optional website and authority confirmation. If interest recording is enabled, we store your indicated USD amount, buy/sell side, company/fund, optional opportunity reference, timestamps, expiry, withdrawal status and aggregate-publication consent. Individual records and investor identities are visible only to you and authorized staff. When public totals are enabled and you agree when submitting, your current amount can contribute to publicly visible company/fund buy or sell interest totals. A total can equal a single contribution when only one person contributes; it is not a promise of anonymity. Prior private submissions are excluded unless reconfirmed with aggregate-publication consent. Restricted-offering-linked indications are excluded from public totals. Expiry or withdrawal removes an amount from the current total; it does not itself delete the record.

**Offering bids.** If bidding is enabled, we store your proposed USD amount, proposed terms, offering and company/fund references, explicit nonbinding confirmation, submission/update timestamps, expiry and withdrawal status. These records are private to you and authorized SVDEX staff; they are not published in company/fund interest totals or automatically disclosed to company representatives. A bid expires after 30 days unless resubmitted. Expiry or withdrawal does not itself delete the record. Submitting a bid does not execute a trade or create an investment commitment.


**Technical activity.** Our site and service providers process information such as IP address, browser/device details, access times, visited pages, search queries, login events, error reports and email delivery information. Cookies and browser storage maintain sessions, preferences and other site functions.

**Company and fund information.** Profiles may contain business contact details, professional names and information supplied by representatives or obtained from publicly available sources. Contact us to request a correction or raise a concern about information relating to you.

## How we use information

We use this information to run and secure the site; create and maintain accounts; publish content; provide search, chat and events; send account messages and notifications; answer requests; moderate misuse; review private-access applications; manage group membership and verification records; and diagnose or improve the service. We may also retain or disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect rights or resolve disputes.

Submitting an application does not automatically grant private access or verify investor eligibility. Application answers are reviewed by authorized staff and are not published as part of your public profile.

## What other people can see

Public posts, public profile fields, company/fund profiles and public achievements can be viewed by visitors, copied and indexed by search engines. Public verification or access achievements can reveal that you have received that status, without publishing your application answers.

Restricted categories and private groups are available to their authorized members and staff. Direct messages and private chats are available to their participants; authorized administrators can access stored content for administration, support, safety or legal purposes. These features are not end-to-end encrypted. Recipients can copy or redistribute information, so do not use the site to store secrets or sensitive documents.

Your account email is not a public profile field. We use it for account administration and the communications described here.

## Service providers and other disclosures

We use Amazon Web Services for hosting, storage, backups and transactional email; Google for optional sign-in and older Google Forms applications; Tally for the private-access questionnaire; and Microsoft Outlook for support correspondence. Providers process information needed to deliver their services and may keep their own security and operational records. Google sign-in and Forms are also subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Tally forms are subject to [Tally's Privacy Policy](https://tally.so/help/privacy-policy).

External links, embedded media and linked offering services may connect to other organizations. Their information handling is governed by their own policies. We do not automatically send private application answers to an issuer or other members simply because you apply; any proposed additional disclosure should be explained separately.

We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not currently use advertising trackers or third-party marketing analytics on SVDEX. Our functional cookies are not switched off by browser Do Not Track signals; blocking cookies may prevent sign-in or other features from working. A service you choose to visit outside SVDEX may handle browser signals differently.

## Storage, retention and security

Our site is hosted in the United States. Google and other providers may process information in other countries. We use HTTPS and restricted administrative access, with encrypted backup storage. No online service can guarantee that information will never be lost or accessed without authorization.

We keep account information and content while needed to provide the service, maintain discussion history, review applications, investigate abuse, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. Some records remain until we delete or anonymize them; there is no automatic expiration for every message or application. Deletion from the live site does not immediately remove copies from backups, email correspondence, search engines or other people's records. Backup copies expire through their retention cycle and are not used as active accounts.

## Your choices and requests

You can edit available profile fields, change notification preferences and manage linked sign-in methods in your account settings. You can stop optional email notifications through those settings or the unsubscribe links where provided; necessary account and security messages may still be sent.

Email [austin@svdex.com](mailto:austin@svdex.com) to request access, a correction, an export, deletion or account anonymization, or to withdraw a private-access application. Tell us which account or information your request concerns. We may verify that you are entitled to make the request. Available rights and exceptions depend on applicable law; some public contributions or necessary records may be retained or anonymized rather than removed. You may also have a right to complain to your local privacy regulator.

## Age and changes

SVDEX is intended for adults and is not directed to children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

We will update this page and its effective date when practices change. For material changes affecting existing accounts, we will provide a notice on the site or by email as appropriate. We will request additional consent when required by applicable law.
